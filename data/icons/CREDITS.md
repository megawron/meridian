## Application icon

The Meridian application icon (`com.martingawron.Meridian.*`) was created with AI image generation and then edited by Martin Gawron.

## Other icons

All other icons in this directory were created with AI assistance. They are original drawings in the Adwaita symbolic style and are not copies of any other icon set that we know of. Individual provenance was not tracked. If you recognize one of them as derived from your work, please [open an issue](https://github.com/megawron/meridian/issues) and it will be corrected or removed.

They are distributed under the same license as Meridian (GPL-3.0-or-later), except for the Adwaita icons listed above.
